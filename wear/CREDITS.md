# Juel's wardrobe: where the pieces come from

Every file here is one piece Juel can wear, fitted to his body and skinned to his skeleton.

## Made for AutoYT

Made from Juel's own body, so they fit him exactly:
`longsleeve.glb` (long-sleeve tee), `tank.glb` (tank top), `beanie.glb` (beanie), `cap.glb` (baseball cap), `headband.glb` (headband).
