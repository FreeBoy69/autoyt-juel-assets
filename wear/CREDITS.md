# Juel's wardrobe: where the pieces come from

Every file here is one piece Juel can wear, fitted to his body and skinned to his skeleton.

## Made for AutoYT

Made from Juel's own body, so they fit him exactly:
`tee.glb` (T-shirt), `longsleeve.glb` (long-sleeve tee), `tank.glb` (tank top), `jacket.glb` (bomber jacket), `dress.glb` (dress), `shorts.glb` (shorts), `trousers.glb` (trousers), `skirt.glb` (skirt), `boots.glb` (boots), `beanie.glb` (beanie), `cap.glb` (baseball cap), `headband.glb` (headband).

## Open-licence models, refitted to Juel

These are other people's models, changed for Juel: fitted to his body and re-skinned to his skeleton. The changes are
ours; the originals are theirs, under the licence shown. Thank you to every author.
